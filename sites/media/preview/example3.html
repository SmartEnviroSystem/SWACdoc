Example 3: Media with and without timestamp

The same sky images, but 13 of them have no timestamp.
The navigation goes by timestamp through the media that have one. The button "Ohne Zeitstempel" shows only the media without timestamp, navigated by id. A second click on the button goes back to the media with timestamp. So no medium is lost.
The images are loaded from the server of the LMU, so an internet connection is needed.
