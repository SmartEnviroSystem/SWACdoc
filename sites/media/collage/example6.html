Example 6: Keogram cut out of the images

With the option keogramFromImage the collage cuts the stripes for the keogram out of the images itself, so no stored stripes are needed. The cut can be changed: its position in the image, its thickness, the width of each stripe in the keogram and the part of the image along the stripe.
A stripe width smaller than the thickness averages the cut pixels, a larger one stretches them. A thick cut with the same stripe width shows a small part of the sky for each image.
Cutting needs the whole images. Here 201 images (one per minute) are loaded from the server of the LMU, so an internet connection is needed and the first drawing takes a while. After that the browser has the images in its cache.

VIEW-Requestor 

<div id="collage_example6" swa="Collage FROM ../../../data/media/collage/example1/skyimages.json WHERE order=ts,ASC AND size=201"></div>
CONFIGURATION

The form calls the function redraw() with the new options. It draws the keogram again without loading the data again.
