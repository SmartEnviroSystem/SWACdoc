Example 7: Switch the datasource

With the option datasources the collage switches to the images of another datasource, e.g. another camera, without reloading the page. Only the images of one datasource are shown at a time.
With showSidebar: true the sidebar offers the datasources for choice (cog icon in the upper right corner, visible when the mouse is over the collage). After a switch timeline or keogram are built again, the display mode is detected again and the jump buttons use jumpSeconds or jumpImages depending on the timestamps of the new images.
Datasources without own size in their fromWheres show as many images as the first datasource.

Switch by code

Pages can switch the datasource without the sidebar, for example from their own camera selection. The component fires swac_collage_example7_datasource_switched when the new images are loaded.

let comp = document.querySelector('#collage_example7').swac_comp;
comp.getDatasources();                                 // [{name, fromName, fromWheres}, ...]
comp.switchDatasource('Sky images without timestamp'); // returns a Promise
