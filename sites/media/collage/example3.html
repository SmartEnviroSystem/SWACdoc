Example 3: Live data from SmartData

Loads the sky images from the collection unimunic_asi of SmartDataHELIOS. SmartData evaluates the filters of the collage, so only the shown images are transferred, even if there are thousands of images.
The function showTimepoint() loads the images beginning at the timepoint from the form.
This example needs the database smartmonitoring_helios and SmartDataHELIOS on the server like in the SmartMonitoringDeveloper environment. If the images do not appear after starting the database, reload the page with clearstates=true.

VIEW-Requestor 

<div id="collage_example3" swa="Collage FROM /SmartDataHELIOS/smartdata/records/unimunic_asi WHERE storage=smartmonitoring AND order=ts,ASC AND size=12 AND includes=id,ts,origin_url"></div>
CONFIGURATION
DATASOURCE

The collage requests the images before and after the shown ones with filters on ts, for example:

/SmartDataHELIOS/smartdata/records/unimunic_asi?storage=smartmonitoring&includes=id,ts,origin_url&filter=ts,gt,2026-06-15T02:01:50&order=ts,asc&size=12
