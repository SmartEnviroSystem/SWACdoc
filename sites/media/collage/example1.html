Example 1: Timeline of all sky images

Sky images of the all sky imager of the LMU Munich from 15.06.2026, one image per minute.
Click a thumbnail or use the arrow keys in the timeline to select an image. The buttons load the previous or next images and jump 30 minutes back or forward. The play button or the space key plays the images like a video and loads the next images automatically.
A static file ignores the filters of the navigation, so the collage filters the delivered datasets itself and shows pageSize images.
The images are loaded from the server of the LMU, so an internet connection is needed.
