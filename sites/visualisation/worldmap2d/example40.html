Example 40: Circle areas, custom icons and area color per dataset

All-Sky-Imager stations observe the sky in a circle around them (areaShape: 'circle'). Datasets can bring their own icon with the attribute map_icon (size over the option mapIconOptions) and can choose the attribute for the fill color of their area with map_areacolorattr, which overwrites the coloring of the ColorSelect plugin. Here the stations in Detmold and Halle are colored by cloud cover, all others by irradiance. The option areaStyle sets the style of the areas (here dark lines).
