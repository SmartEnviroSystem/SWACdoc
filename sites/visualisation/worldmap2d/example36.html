Example 36: Color markers by an attribute chosen by the administrator

With the option colorAttr the administrator decides which attribute of the datasets determines the color of the markers (and areas). The colors are defined once in the Datadescription component. Colors without a matching marker icon (e.g. from gradients) are drawn as SVG markers.
The option can also be set per datasource: datasources.get(NAME).colorAttr.
