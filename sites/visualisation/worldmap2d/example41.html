Example 41: Satellite raster without markers

Raster data like satellite data has a value for every cell of a grid. Here are 20 x 20 cells of Meteosat data between Munich and Rosenheim (30.06.2025, 12:04). With areaOnly: true only the cells are drawn and no markers, which keeps maps with thousands of cells clear and fast. A click on a cell works like a click on a marker, so the DataShowModal plugin shows its values. The grid of satellite data is often given in degrees, with areaSizeDeg (size in degrees [longitude, latitude]) the cells fit together without gaps. Colors come from the Datadescription component (calcmode gradient), with the ColorSelect plugin the user can switch between visible light (clouds are bright) and infrared.
