Example 18: TableHierarchy Plugin

TableHierarchy groups related rows below an expandable parent row. Configure relations with parentId and childId, or provide an async loadRelations function. Expand a parent row to see all its descendants; independent rows remain visible.

Sorting keeps groups together. Column filters combine with AND and show the complete group when a member matches. Clear the filters to restore the previous expanded state. With linear: true, branching or cyclic groups stay flat with a warning. expandWhen(dataset) keeps a group open while any member matches. Entry 2 has highlighted: true, so its group opens automatically and cannot be collapsed. Uncheck the option to return to manual expansion. Call the plugin's render() after external state changes.
